- first third of the run needed **3.82s**, last third needed **3.88s**, a change of +0.05s.
- **a player configured from this run's opening third would eventually stall**, by 0.05s. That is what every buffer recommendation in `profiles.md` was derived from, since each of those runs is roughly one third of this one long.

## The longest a viewer waited

- the feed named the same newest segment for **3.50s**, across 2 poll(s), starting 2.7 minutes in.
- the bench itself went at most 2.28s between two polls, against a 2.00s cadence. Nothing shorter than that is observable here.
- **that stall is the feed's**: the bench asked 2 times inside it and got the same answer every time, so a viewer polling at this cadence saw the stream stop for that long.
- a player holding 6.38s would have played through it.
- when it moved, the gateway's resolved feed index jumped **1 updates**. A reader keeping up moves one at a time, so that many were already written and waiting: the writer did not pause, the reader was behind it.
