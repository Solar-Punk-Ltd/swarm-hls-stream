## Does the buffer demand grow

- first third of the run needed **1.81s**, last third needed **1.87s**, a change of +0.07s.
- **a player configured from this run's opening third would eventually stall**, by 0.07s. That is what every buffer recommendation in `profiles.md` was derived from, since each of those runs is roughly one third of this one long.

## The longest a viewer waited

- the feed named the same newest segment for **1.17s**, across 1 poll(s), starting 0.1 minutes in.
- the bench itself went at most 1.17s between two polls, against a 2.00s cadence. Nothing shorter than that is observable here.
- **that gap is this instrument's, not the feed's.** Only one poll fell inside it, so the feed may have advanced any number of times unobserved. Read it as the resolution of the measurement rather than as a stall.
- whether a player would have rebuffered cannot be said from this run, for the reason above.
- when it moved, the gateway's resolved feed index jumped **1 updates**. A reader keeping up moves one at a time, so that many were already written and waiting: the writer did not pause, the reader was behind it.
